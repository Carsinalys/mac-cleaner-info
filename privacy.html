Privacy Policy

MacCleaner is a macOS app that scans your home folder for disk space wasters and helps you delete them safely. It collects no personal data, contains no analytics or advertising code, and works entirely on your Mac.

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Applies to MacCleaner 1.0 and later.

Summary

What this policy comes down to

  • MacCleaner collects no personal data whatsoever.
  • It does not track you across apps, websites or devices.
  • File names, sizes, and scan results are used only to display them to you and are never stored outside your Mac.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no third-party SDK.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own. The only network traffic is Apple's own App Store purchase verification, performed by macOS.

How file scanning works

To scan for caches, duplicates, and large files, macOS requires that you explicitly grant MacCleaner access to your home folder. The app reads file metadata (names, sizes, dates) within the folders you grant. When you run the duplicate finder, it reads the contents of files of the same size to compute checksums, and the similar-photo finder decodes images to compare them. Files that are only stored in iCloud or another cloud service are not read. This data is:

  • Used only on your Mac to produce the scan results you see.
  • Held in memory only during the scan. When you close the app or start a new scan, previous results are replaced. A small local cache of file hashes lets repeat scans run faster; it is removed by the "Reset All App Data" button in Settings.
  • Never written to any server, log, or third-party service.
  • Not used to build any profile of you. MacCleaner has no concept of a "user" — there is nothing to identify you with.

The Uninstaller reads app names, bundle identifiers, versions, sizes, and paths from your Applications folders. If you choose to remove an app, macOS may ask you to grant access to that app's data; with your permission, MacCleaner reads matching support, preference, cache, and container files in your home Library folder to find leftovers. macOS may also ask for access to other apps' data; if you decline, some leftovers may not be listed. This information is used only to show scan results and is never uploaded.

What is stored on your Mac

The app saves its own preferences locally inside its sandbox container:

  • scan thresholds (large file minimum size, old downloads age);
  • feature switches (Smart Clean preferences, quarantine retention);
  • which folders you've granted access to — for example, your home folder or Applications folder — as security-scoped bookmarks, so you don't have to re-grant access every launch;
  • which paths you've excluded from scans;
  • a local audit log of deletions — so you can see what was cleaned and when;
  • a local hash cache — so repeat scans skip files that haven't changed.

None of this is personal data, none of it is transmitted, and all of it is removed when you delete the app. The "Reset All App Data" button in Settings (→ Reset) also removes all of it without deleting the app.

Purchases

The Lifetime License is an Apple in-app purchase, handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. Payment is processed by Apple; the developer never sees or receives your name, email address, payment card or billing details.

The app reads Apple's cryptographically signed purchase record on your device to determine whether the license is active. That record stays on your Mac and in your Apple Account. Apple's own privacy policy governs the purchase itself: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Third parties and tracking

MacCleaner contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or attribution SDKs. No data is shared with, sold to or brokered through any third party. There are no cookies or trackers in the app. Nothing is combined with data from other companies to build a profile of you.

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which — like any web host — may process standard server request data such as your IP address and browser type in order to serve the page. That processing is GitHub's, described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site loads no analytics, no advertising and no third-party fonts.

If you email support

Contacting support is optional. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. It is used only to answer you, is not shared with anyone, and is deleted on request. Email is handled by the support mailbox's email provider under its own privacy terms. Please don't send file lists or logs containing information you consider private.

Your rights, retention and deletion

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing held about you to access, correct, export, restrict or erase, and no data is retained on any server. In practice:

  • Deleting your data: remove the app from your Applications folder. Your locally stored preferences and scan results go with it. Or use Settings → Reset → Reset All App Data to wipe everything without deleting the app.
  • Revoking permission: the app only accesses folders you explicitly granted. You can re-grant, change, or remove access at any time from Settings → Folder Access.
  • Purchase history: managed by Apple through your Apple Account.

If you are in the EU/EEA, the UK, California or another region with statutory data rights, you may still contact us at cardinalys81@gmail.com and we will confirm in writing that no personal data about you is held.

Children

MacCleaner is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no advertising, no user-generated content and no links to social networks.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date above will change. The current privacy commitments — no collection, no tracking, no network use beyond Apple's purchase checks — are part of the product, not a temporary position.
